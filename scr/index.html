Introduction

🚉 What is SCR?

Stepford County Railway (SCR) is a British-inspired heavy rail simulation on Roblox, open since 25 November 2017. Players drive trains, dispatch, guard, or signal across a UK-style network. Founded by SCRTeamOfficial (Charlie_RBX, visually impaired); BanTech became the main developer.

Infrastructure is maintained jointly by SCR and Transport for Stepford (TfS). All five operators run 100 routes. The game has 10 fare zones. AirLink and Express offer First Class.

🎓 Training Process

Dedicated training map used for all trainings since V1.10.4. Training routes R901–R905 are used. Signaller Training 2.0 will move the process fully in-game (no Discord/Google Forms required).

1
Trainee Driver → Qualified Driver
Earn 120 XP. Attend a scheduled QD training session. Pass the practical assessment.
120 XP · SCR Group membership
2
Qualified Driver → Dispatcher
Earn 360 XP. Pass Dispatcher theory quiz. Attend Dispatcher training on the dedicated training map. Learn platform positioning and signal protocol.
QD rank · 360 XP · theory quiz · DS training
3
Dispatcher → Guard
Attend and pass a Guard training session. Learn door operations, communication with driver and dispatcher.
Dispatcher rank · GD training
4
Guard → Signaller
Submit application in SCR Official Discord (#application-forms). Applications open periodically. If accepted, complete practical training session. Theory was historically a separate test — merged into application questions since Sept 2021.
Guard/SV rank · 13+ · PC/laptop · SCR Official Discord
5
→ Supervisor (any dept)
Apply via Discord when open for your target department. Pass Supervisor training. Requires ClickUp/Google/Roblox/Discord accounts with 2FA. May join up to two departments.
QD+ (Driving) · Guard+ (Dispatching/Signalling) · 2FA on all accounts · PC + reliable internet
🔮Training 2.0 (Signaller): Full in-game training process planned — removes Discord/age requirements and introduces simulated virtual traffic for consistent difficulty across all trainees.
Conduct

📋 General Rules

  • 🚫Exploiting/Hacking: Any third-party tools for unfair advantage. Permanent ban. Accessing staff areas (cabs, ROCs, signal boxes, offices) via exploits is covered here.
  • 🚫SPAD: Driving past a red signal without Signaller authorisation.
  • 🚫Trolling: Disrupting services, blocking trains, griefing, or train surfing.
  • 🚫Trespassing: Walking on/alongside tracks, embankments, or railway bridges. Level crossings excepted.
  • 🚫Impersonation: Claiming to be a staff member or another player.
  • ✅Station Parkour: Climbing roofs, gantries, OLE equipment. Permitted since 12 Feb 2022.
  • ✅Platform Jumping: Within stations. Permitted since 12 Feb 2022.
  • ✅Grass/Baseplate: Freely accessible since V1.6.
Key Rule

⛔ Trespassing

🚧Current definition: Walking on or alongside train tracks (except level crossings), embankments, or the trackside portion of railway bridges.

Version Timeline

📅 Update History

25 Nov 2017
Public Launch (Beta)
Single route: Stepford Town ↔ Port Benton. Only Connect and the Class 700. Very under-detailed network.
The Big Update — V1.0
Complete Overhaul · Out of Beta
Points system, headcode system, anti-trespassing script. Signallers renamed from Controllers. First 20 Signallers recruited. Waterline removed from gamepass.
Version 1.1
AirLink + 13 New Stations
AirLink introduced. Platforms 12–15 at Stepford Central (inspired by London Waterloo). Airport Terminals 1 & 2 added.
Version 1.4
Stepford Express + Terminal 3
Express added as 4th operator. Stepford Central renovated (now also based on Manchester Piccadilly). Terminal 3 added.
Version 1.5
Connect Rebrand · Waterline Spin-Off · Guard Role
Connect becomes "Stepford Connect" (sky-blue #0096EE). Waterline spun off from Connect. Guard rank introduced.
Version 1.6
Baseplate Walking Permitted
Anti-trespass script amended. Grasslands/vegetation now accessible. Connect/Waterline NGv1 upgrades.
Version 1.7
Express Rebrand · NGv3 Begins
Express rebranded (last operator to do so). NGv3 model standard introduced. Class 377 updated to /7 subclass, 5 coaches.
Version 1.8 / 1.8.2
Class 717 · Training Centre
Class 717 to Connect. SCR Training Centre built near Stepford United FC (superseded by V1.10.4 training map).
Version 1.9
Express to Newry Harbour · Esterfield Extension
Express extended: R083–R086 added. Announced on April Fools — initially dismissed as a joke. Waterline extended to Esterfield (Farleigh, Rosedale Village added).
Version 1.10 / 1.10.4
Class 345 · Dedicated Training Map
Class 345 introduced (4,500 pts — priciest in SCR). Dedicated training map replaces in-game venues. Training routes R901–R905.
12 February 2022
Trespassing Redefined
Station parkour/platform jumping decriminalised. Train surfing → trolling. Staff-only area violations → exploit rule.
12 August 2024 — V2.0
New Movement System · Rayleigh Bay Branch
Realistic acceleration/deceleration physics. Revamped driving & dispatching UIs. Rayleigh Bay Branch added. All Connect/Waterline trains → NGv3.
11 May 2025 — V2.2
Metro — 5th Operator
First new operator in 5+ years. 12 Connect routes + Class 717 → Metro. Metro Core tunnel opened. Extensions to Stepford Bay & Whitney Green. 7-car & 8-car Class 43 variants added.
10 January 2026 — V2.3
Power States · Stepford Bay Branch
First ever power states in SCR. Bi-mode/tri-mode functional. Battery trains charge on other power sources. Emergency brake when pantograph raised leaving OLE. New Stepford Bay branch. Class 387 returned to Connect (left in 2017). 23 sneak peeks released — 2nd most in SCR history.
